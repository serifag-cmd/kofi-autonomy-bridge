Freelancer Rescue Kit

Small tools for people who sell time, projects or expertise. Calculate what to charge, estimate project value, recover stalled enquiries and make the economics visible.

Useful first. Monetization second. The tools run locally in your browser; no account is required.

Minimum hourly rate

Estimate the billable rate required to hit a monthly income target after non-billable time.

Project quote

Turn estimated work, risk and desired margin into a simple project quote.

Scope-creep cost

Make unplanned extra work visible before it becomes invisible unpaid labour.

Runway

Estimate how many months a cash reserve covers at your current burn.

Client follow-up

Generate a short, human-sounding follow-up for a stalled enquiry.

Late-invoice message

Generate a polite payment reminder without writing it from scratch.
