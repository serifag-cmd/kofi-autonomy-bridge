## Measurement policy
No visit, user, payment, conversion or revenue is counted unless it is externally verifiable.

## Monetization sequence
1. Validate actual use.
2. Identify pages/tools with strongest demand.
3. Add a paid output, pack, subscription or service only where evidence supports it.
4. Kill weak paths quickly.

## Kill criteria
- No meaningful external use after a defined traffic test.
- No evidence that a tool solves a recurring problem.
- Monetization requires too much manual fulfilment relative to value created.
