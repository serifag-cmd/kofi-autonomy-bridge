# EVEREST ZERO

## Competitive track

EVEREST ZERO is the autonomous track of the EVEREST experiment.

### Rule

The assistant chooses the opportunity, builds the product, launches the experiment, measures real-world signal, iterates, and records every human intervention that is genuinely required.

Human intervention is not prohibited; it is counted.

### Competition

Track A: EVEREST HUMAN+AI
- Existing human + AI economic engine.
- Current hypothesis: AI-assisted workflow automation for service businesses.

Track B: EVEREST ZERO
- AI-selected and AI-operated opportunity.
- Starting with assets and infrastructure that can be built and maintained through the currently authorized toolset.
- No manual business selection by the human participant.

### Scoreboard

Primary:
1. Real revenue received.
2. Net economic value after direct experiment costs.
3. Human interventions required.
4. Human minutes per € of net value.
5. Time from idea to first public test.
6. Repeatability of the acquisition/fulfilment loop.

Secondary:
- visits
- qualified actions
- leads
- payments
- conversion rate
- fulfilment time
- failures
- refunds
- recurring revenue, where applicable

### Integrity

No simulated sales count as sales.
No traffic generated by the test environment counts as customer demand.
No unverified deployment counts as launched.
No forecast counts as revenue.
Every external claim is sourced.
Every blocked capability is recorded as a boundary rather than hidden.

## Initial hypothesis

EVEREST ZERO starts with a low-cost digital utility/product model designed to minimize external dependencies and allow rapid iteration.

The first product will be a free, useful browser utility suite for freelancers and small operators, with an optional paid/support path. The exact monetization mechanism remains an experiment, not an assumption.

## First milestone

Produce a public, usable MVP and obtain the first external demand signal without requiring the human participant to choose the product, write the code, or operate the experiment.
